## Remaining Observations

### Test Coverage Gaps (Not Bugs)
The following areas have low test coverage but require mock infrastructure (Redis/DB/centrifuge) that would be a significant effort to set up:
- `internal/agent/agent.go` (New, applyHelperDefaults, initialize)
- `internal/agent/attachment*.go` (AttachmentManager, BuildAttachments)
- `internal/agent/data_context.go` (loadMessages, convertDBMessage sub-functions)
- `internal/agent/reactive_compact.go` (Level 1/2/3 - require DB mocks)
- `pkg/turn-agent/agent_process.go` (Process - requires full agent setup)

These are not bugs but represent future improvement opportunities. The current test coverage focuses on pure functions and business logic that can be tested without external dependencies.

### Design Choices (Not Issues)
- `events.Next()` idle warning is warning-only (no forced exit) because eino's `AsyncIterator` does not support `Close()/Cancel()`. Mitigated by `staleTurnScanner` periodic recovery.
- Checkpoint TTL is 24h (not dynamic based on session activity). This is intentional to avoid complexity; stale interrupted turns are recovered by the scanner after 30 minutes.
- Turn/Session status updates are not transactional (DB update + publish are independent). This is by design: DB update must succeed, publish is best-effort.
